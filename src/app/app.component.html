🛠️ Mantra MFS110 Quick Fix & Setup Guide

  1. USB Plug: Ensure Mantra MFS110 scanner is plugged into a USB port on your PC.
  2. Windows RD Service: Press Win + R → type services.msc → locate "Mantra RD Service" or "MFS110 RD Service" and verify status is Running.
  3. Browser Test: Open http://127.0.0.1:11100/ in your browser. If you see the RD Service XML status, the driver is working properly.
  4. Need Drivers? Download the official MFS110 driver and RD Service from the Mantra Softech Download Portal.
